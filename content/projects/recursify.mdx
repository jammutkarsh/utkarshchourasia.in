Solving a LeetCode problem once doesn't mean you can solve it again in an interview a
month later. Re-solving at the right time is what makes a pattern stick, but keeping
track of what to revisit, and when, by hand quickly turns into a spreadsheet nobody
updates.

Recursify schedules the re-solves for you with FSRS spaced repetition. You grade each
attempt from Forgot to Instant and the next review moves to match, while a topic polygon
shows at a glance which areas are slipping. Blind 75, LeetCode 75 and NeetCode 150 are
built in, alongside your own list, and the whole app is keyboard-first.

It's free and self-hosted: you fork it and deploy it to your own Cloudflare account with
a D1 database, so your data never leaves it. The [demo](https://demo.recursify.utkarshchourasia.in)
runs without sign-up and keeps its data in your browser.
