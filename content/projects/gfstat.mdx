GitHub shows your followers and the people you follow as two separate lists, and gives
no way to compare them. Working out who doesn't follow you back means paging through
both by hand.

GFStat signs you in with GitHub and compares the two lists for you: mutuals, people who
don't follow you back, and people you don't follow back. You can follow or unfollow
straight from the results.

It is a server-rendered SvelteKit app on Cloudflare Pages, with sessions kept in
Cloudflare KV and plain `fetch` calls against the GitHub REST API. The UI uses my own
design system, utc-ds.
